Where we are located

Plants and offices

We are integrated through 6 manufacturing plants in Colombia with a vertical system that covers spinning, dyeing, textiles, clothing and hosiery processes. We have administrative offices in Colombia, the United States, Mexico and Costa Rica.

Distribution Centers

We have 3 distribution centers to ensure flexibility, competitiveness and efficiency.

Stores

We have a wide distribution of our products in our own stores and franchises located in Central America, the Caribbean and South America.
